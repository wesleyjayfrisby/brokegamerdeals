Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Broke Gamer Deals ("we", "us") runs the Broke Gamer Deals channels on Instagram, Telegram and YouTube and this website. This policy explains what information our website and our publishing tool ("BGD Poster") handle.

Website visitors

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics, ads or tracking scripts, and we do not collect any personal information from visitors. GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

BGD Poster and YouTube API Services

BGD Poster is an internal tool that publishes our own daily deal videos (Shorts) to our own YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube API Services. It is used only by the Broke Gamer Deals account owner; it is not offered to the public and no other users sign in to it.

When the account owner authorises BGD Poster, Google provides an access token limited to the youtube.upload permission. The tool uses it only to upload our own videos and set their title, description and tags. We do not access, collect, store or share any other YouTube or Google account data, and we do not access data about viewers or other channels.

The authorisation token is stored privately in our own secured workspace and is never shared or sold. You can revoke BGD Poster's access at any time on the Google security settings page.

By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Affiliate links

Some deal links are affiliate links. If you buy through them, the store may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. The stores and affiliate networks use their own cookies under their own privacy policies; we receive only aggregate sales reports, not your personal details.

Children

Our content is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
